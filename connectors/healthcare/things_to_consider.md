1. The "Many CCNs per Physical Location" ProblemThe Risk: CMS issues CCNs based on billing and certification structure, not physical real estate. A large medical center campus often has separate CCNs for its acute care unit, its psychiatric wing, and its inpatient rehab unit.The Impact: If you map strictly 1:1 by CCN, your database will show 3–5 different "facilities" sitting on the exact same physical piece of land, artificially inflating your account count.The Fix: Group your joined CMS data by Physical Address + ZIP code before final delivery to establish a true physical "site" record.2. The CMS Ownership PitfallThe Risk: The CMS Hospital All Owners file tracks legal ownership for Medicare transparency. It is notoriously messy, filled with shell corporations (e.g., "JV Holdings LLC") rather than the consumer-facing brand (e.g., "Ascension Health").The Impact: Your "system rollup" will be fractured and unmarketable.The Fix: Use the NPPES V2 Authorized Official or Provider Secondary Identifiers fields to look for parent health system names, or use commercial crosswalks to map legal entities to health system brands.3. State Supplement StandardizationThe Risk: Every state categorizes senior living differently. NC DHSR uses different columns and definitions than PA or FL.The Impact: Hardcoding a pipeline for NC will fail when applied to PA's schema.The Fix: Build a strict, minimal target schema for state data (e.g., FACILITY_NAME, STREET, CITY, STATE, ZIP, CAPACITY_BEDS, FACILITY_TYPE_RAW). Map state exports into this common schema before merging them with the master spine.4. The NPPES Filter TrapThe Risk: Filtering NPPES strictly by ENTITY_TYPE_CODE=2 (Organizations) and "facility taxonomies" will exclude off-campus clinics and massive ambulatory surgery centers if they are registered under a medical group taxonomy instead of a hospital taxonomy.The Impact: You miss high-value medical office buildings (MOBs) that require independent grounds maintenance.The Fix: Expand taxonomy filters to include Ambulatory Health Care (261Q...) and Specialist Medical Clinics.🛠️ Strategic Enhancements to ConsiderAdd the CMS Hospital Market Basket (POS Extended): Ensure you are extracting the "Control Type" code from the POS file. This explicitly tells you if a facility is Non-Profit, For-Profit, or Government-Owned, which dictates their procurement and bidding cycles.Geocoding Step: Because your use case implies physical asset management (grounds-spend), run all finalized addresses through a geocoder. CMS addresses frequently contain typos or PO Boxes that cause mapping failures.
